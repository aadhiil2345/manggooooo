About the Mango Plant

Mango is a tropical evergreen fruit tree known scientifically as Mangifera indica. The planted mango belongs to the Anacardiaceae family and is loved for its broad shade, glossy leaves, fragrant flowers, and sweet fruit.

It grows best in warm, sunny climates with rich, well-drained soil. A healthy mango tree can become a generous shade tree and later produce golden, green, or blush-colored fruits with juicy flesh.

  • Type Tropical evergreen fruit tree
  • Family Anacardiaceae
  • Climate Warm, sunny, frost-free
  • Care Sunlight, water, mulch, patience
